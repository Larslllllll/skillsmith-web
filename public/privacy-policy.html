Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09.

What this site does with the text you paste

The free scanner at /api/scan and the paid batch endpoint at /api/scan-pro process the SKILL.md text you submit entirely in-memory, for the duration of a single request, to run the lint and security-scan checks. We do not log, store, retain, or share the content you paste. Nothing you submit is written to a database, file, or third-party service by this site's own code.

Server logs

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may retain standard, short-lived infrastructure request logs (e.g. IP address, timestamp, URL requested, status code) for operational and security purposes, per Vercel's privacy policy. We do not separately collect, sell, or analyze this data ourselves.

Payments (Pro tier)

The Pro batch-scan API is paid via a public, on-chain USDC transfer on Solana. We verify your payment by reading the public Solana blockchain (a public ledger, not something we control or that identifies you personally beyond your wallet address). We do not collect names, emails, or card details for this flow — there is no account or signup.

Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and measure their performance. We do not control what Google does with this data beyond what is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

We use Google's Consent Mode: the base AdSense script is present on every page (as Google's own setup requires), but by default no ad or analytics cookie is stored and no personalized ad is served -- that only happens after you accept the consent banner shown on first visit. You can change your choice at any time via the "Cookie / ad consent settings" link in the footer, or opt out of personalized advertising globally via Google's Ad Settings or aboutads.info.

Analytics

This site does not run its own analytics/tracking script. Any usage statistics available to us come solely from Vercel's own hosting-level metrics and, where consented to, Google AdSense's ad-performance reporting.

Your choices

  • Manage or withdraw ad-cookie consent: re-open the consent banner by clearing this site's local storage, or visit Google Ad Settings.
  • Do not paste anything you consider sensitive — while we don't store it, treat any third-party web service the same way you'd treat any tool you don't control end-to-end.
